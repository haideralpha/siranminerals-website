Calcite

CaCO3: 97%-99.5%

Applications: Plastics, masterbatch, ceramics, paints, paper, glass

Forms: Lumps, crushed, powder, subject to specification

Talc

Brightness: 92%-98%

Applications: Ceramics, plastics, paint, rubber, paper coating

Notes: Lamellar structure for lubrication and surface finish

Antimony Ore

Sb: 20%-60%

Applications: Alloys, battery grids, flame retardant chemicals, metallurgy

Chromite Ore

Cr2O3: 30%-48%

Applications: Ferrochrome, refractory manufacturing, foundry sand

Note: Cr:Fe ratio confirmed per buyer requirement

Barite

BaSO4: 90%-98%

Applications: Oil and gas drilling, paints, plastics, rubber, chemicals

Forms: Lumps, crushed, powder, subject to specification

Bauxite

Al2O3: 45%-60%

Applications: Cement, refractories, alumina production, abrasives

Forms: Lumps and crushed material

Copper Ore

Cu: 5%-25%, depending on grade

Applications: Metallurgical processing, alloys, industrial manufacturing

Forms: Lumps or concentrate, subject to availability

Lead Ore

Pb: 40%-70%, depending on grade

Applications: Battery manufacturing, alloys, radiation shielding, metallurgical processing

Forms: Lumps or concentrate, subject to availability

Feldspar

K2O + Na2O: 10%-15%, depending on grade

Applications: Ceramics, glass manufacturing, fillers, enamel production

Forms: Lumps or powder, subject to availability

Iron Ore

Fe: 45%-65%, depending on grade

Applications: Steel production, construction, industrial manufacturing

Forms: Lumps, fines, or concentrate, subject to availability

Quartz
