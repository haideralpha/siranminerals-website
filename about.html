About Siran Rocks & Minerals

A Pakistan sourcing partner focused on buyer confidence

We coordinate mineral sourcing, verification, documentation, and shipment planning for overseas manufacturers, processors, and traders buying from Pakistan.

Our Role

Siran Rocks & Minerals operates as a coordination bridge between mines, processors, laboratories, logistics providers, and international buyers. Our work starts with a technical requirement and ends with a shipment plan that reflects grade, quantity, packing, inspection, documentation, and trade terms.

We focus on industrial minerals, metallurgical ores, selected rare earth mineral requirements, and natural gemstones sourced through regional networks across Pakistan.

Buyer assurance

  • Specification alignment before quotation
  • Lab report and COA coordination where available
  • Third-party inspection support on request
  • Clear export documentation process
  • Direct WhatsApp and email communication

Pakistan's Mineral Advantage

Pakistan hosts commercially relevant deposits of calcite, talc, chromite, antimony, barite, bauxite, copper-bearing material, and high-value gemstones from northern mountain belts. For international buyers, the opportunity is strongest when sourcing is handled through disciplined specification review and transparent shipment coordination.

How We Reduce Procurement Risk

01Define the requirement

Product, grade, chemistry, sizing, moisture, packing, and destination are confirmed first.

02Match the source

Mine and processor options are screened against the buyer's technical and commercial needs.

03Verify before shipment

Samples, lab reports, buyer inspection, or SGS inspection can be coordinated where feasible.

04Execute export terms

FOB, CFR, and CIF quotations are coordinated through Karachi Port or Port Qasim.

Export Terms and Documentation

FOB

We coordinate sourcing, packing, local movement, port handling, and loading support under Free on Board terms.

CFR / CIF

Freight, insurance where applicable, and destination port delivery planning can be quoted after route and cargo confirmation.

Documents

Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, bill of lading, and inspection documents can be coordinated according to shipment needs.
