About Siran Minerals

Siran Minerals is a Pakistan-based mineral sourcing and export coordination company working with mining regions across northern Pakistan and mineral belts nationwide. The company supports international buyers with structured procurement of industrial minerals, metallurgical ores, and collector-grade gemstones based on specification feasibility and shipment planning requirements.

Focus on reliability

We prioritize consistency, traceability, and compliance across every link of the supply chain—from mine sourcing to documentation and shipment.

Grade-based sourcing

Our procurement process emphasizes grade confirmation and specification adherence to ensure clients receive materials that meet exact technical requirements.

Industrial supply continuity

Through long-term partnerships, we maintain stable access to core mineral categories, enabling reliable supply for ongoing production cycles.

Long-term trade partnerships

We aim to build enduring collaborations with manufacturers, processors, and traders to support strategic growth and global competitiveness.

Pakistan’s Mineral Advantage

Pakistan hosts globally recognized reserves across key minerals including calcite, talc, chromite, antimony, and pegmatite gemstones. Our network leverages these regions to secure dependable supply aligned with international market standards.
