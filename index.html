Core Products Overview

Calcite

CaCO₃: 97%–99.5%

Applications: Plastics, Masterbatch, Ceramics, Paints, Paper, Glass

Available forms: Lumps, Crushed, Powder (subject to specification)

Talc

Brightness: 85%–92%

Applications: Ceramics, Plastics, Paint, Rubber, Paper Coating

Notes: Lamellar structure provides lubrication and brightness advantages

Antimony Ore

Sb content: 25%–60%

Applications: Alloys, Battery grids, Flame retardant chemicals, Metallurgical processing

Chromite Ore

Applications: Ferrochrome production, Refractory manufacturing, Foundry sand

Note: Cr:Fe ratio importance in alloying
